For buyers

Buying directly, done simply.

No account, no middlemen — just you, the artist, and the piece you love.

Why buy directly

When a piece is bought through a gallery or a large platform, a significant share of the price rarely reaches the person who made it. Buying directly means more of what you pay goes to the artist — and you know exactly whose hands made the thing on your wall.

Direct

Every enquiry goes straight to the artist — no gallery mark-up sitting between you and them.

Independent

Every artist here is independent — no galleries, no middlemen, just the person who made the work.

Known

Each artist's page tells you who made the work, where, and what other buyers thought.

How it works

First

Browse the gallery

Explore work by artist, or browse the full curated gallery to see everything available right now.

Then

Enquire about a piece

"Enquire about this piece" opens an email addressed to the artist, with the piece already noted, and The Screen Gallery copied in.

Finally

Arrange it together

The artist replies directly to confirm payment and delivery or collection.
