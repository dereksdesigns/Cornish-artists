Susan Lock

Living and painting in Cornwall, I am a contemporary artist with a passion for colour, texture and the ever-changing natural world. Working primarily in acrylics, acrylic inks, oils and oil pastels, I create semi-abstract, expressive landscapes and seascapes inspired by the beauty and atmosphere of the Cornish coast and countryside.

After retiring early from a career in Social Work, I rediscovered my love of painting at an art workshop in Malpas, near Truro. What began as a three-hour session quickly became an all-consuming passion — one that has shaped the last five years of my life.

I love exploring the hidden colours, tones and textures of the world around me, looking beyond the obvious to find the extraordinary in the everyday. For me, painting is more than creating an image; it is an immersive, deeply personal experience that brings joy, focus and a profound sense of connection with my surroundings.

My work is expressive, intuitive and full of feeling, reflecting my ongoing fascination with the landscape I am fortunate enough to call home.

I paint not simply what I see, but what I feel — capturing the spirit, energy and ever-changing beauty of Cornwall.
