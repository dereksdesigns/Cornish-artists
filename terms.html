Terms & policies

How buying here works.

The essentials, in plain language, before you buy.

Payment

All purchases are paid directly to The Screen Gallery via bank transfer. Once your enquiry is confirmed, we'll send you payment details and a reference to include with your transfer.

Shipping & delivery

Each piece is shipped directly by the artist using a tracked and signed-for delivery service. Once your order is dispatched, we'll email you the tracking details so you can follow its journey.

Returns & the 14-day window

Under UK consumer law, you have a 14-day statutory right to change your mind on most online purchases. If you'd like to return a piece within this window, please contact us as soon as possible at thescreengallery.shop@gmail.com. Please note: a 10% commission on the sale, and the original delivery fee, are non-refundable in the event of a return. Full refunds (including these amounts) will be issued in cases of damage in transit or significant misrepresentation of the piece.

Originality

Every piece sold through The Screen Gallery is an original work created by the listed artist. We do not sell prints, reproductions, or third-party work without clear disclosure.

Liability in transit

Artwork is shipped directly by the artist, who is responsible for secure packaging and appropriate insurance. If your piece arrives damaged, contact us immediately so we can help resolve this with the artist.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you have a question about a specific order, reach out any time at thescreengallery.shop@gmail.com.
