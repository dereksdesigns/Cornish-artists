A marketplace for Cornish artists

Art made alongthis coastline, sold directly to you.

Cornwall has drawn painters, potters and printmakers for well over a century — from the light that once filled the studios of St Ives to the working harbours of Newlyn. Cornwall Makers is a place to meet the artists working here now, and to buy their work directly.

Why buying local, from the artist, still matters

When a piece is bought through a gallery or a large platform, a significant share of the price rarely reaches the person who made it. Buying directly means more of what you pay goes to the artist — and you get to know whose hands made the thing on your wall.

Direct

Every enquiry on this site goes straight to the artist — no gallery mark-up sitting between you and them.
