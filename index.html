About us

A gallery without walls

The Screen Gallery represents independent artists — we manage the platform, promote their work, and handle every sale, so they can focus on making it.

Every piece in our gallery is original — abstract, coastal-inspired, hand-made work born from real experience, texture, and story. This isn't stock art or prints; it's art made by people who've lived the moments behind it, and you can feel that in every brushstroke.

We work with independent artists — people who've turned careers, setbacks, and lifelong passions into a body of work. The Screen Gallery helps them reach buyers they wouldn't otherwise find, handling the website, sales, and promotion, so they can focus on what they do best: creating.

Traditional galleries come with real barriers — expensive wall space, steep commission rates, and limited visibility. The Screen Gallery removes those walls: lower costs, wider exposure, and a platform that's genuinely accessible to any independent artist ready to share their work with the world.
