What The Screen Gallery is

The Screen Gallery is a digital gallery where independent artists sell original work directly to buyers. Behind every piece is someone who poured hours, doubt, and hope into making it. We just help you find them — no gallery mark-up, no middlemen, just the work and the person who made it.

I started this because talented artists are too often shut out by limited gallery space, steep commission rates, and the hassle of building their own online store. This is a simpler way for artists to sell directly, and for buyers to know exactly whose work they're bringing home.
