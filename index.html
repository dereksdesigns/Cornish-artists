Why this site exists

I started The Screen Gallery because talented artists are too often shut out by limited gallery space, steep commission rates, and the hassle of building their own online store. This is a simpler way for artists to sell directly, and for buyers to know exactly whose work they're bringing home.

Direct

Every purchase goes straight to the artist — no gallery mark-up sitting between you and them.

Independent

Every artist here is independent — no galleries, no middlemen, just the person who made the work.

Known

Each artist has their own page telling you who made the work and what buyers thought.
