A marketplace for Cornish artists

Art made alongthis coastline, sold directly to you.

Cornwall has drawn painters, potters and printmakers for well over a century — from the light that once filled the studios of St Ives to the working harbours of Newlyn. Cornwall Makers is a place to meet the artists working here now, and to buy their work directly.

Why this site exists

I started Cornwall Makers because so much brilliant work by local artists never reaches the people who'd love to buy it — gallery space is limited, commission rates can be steep, and getting online often means fighting with a big, impersonal platform. This is a smaller, more accessible way for Cornish artists to sell directly, and for buyers to know exactly whose work they're bringing home.
